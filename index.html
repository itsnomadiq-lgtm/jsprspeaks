jspr.speaks Publisher

jspr.speaks Publisher is the small tool we use to upload our own finished videos to the jspr.speaks YouTube and Instagram accounts and to read how those videos perform. It signs in with Google (YouTube) and Instagram, and it only acts on the jspr.speaks accounts. It is not offered to the public.

How it handles data is described in our Privacy Policy. Use of this site and the tool is covered by our Terms of Service.
