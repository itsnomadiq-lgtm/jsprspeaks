Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

This policy covers the jspr.speaks website and jspr.speaks Publisher, the tool we use to publish our own videos. "We" means the jspr.speaks channel.

What jspr.speaks Publisher does

jspr.speaks Publisher uploads videos we made to the jspr.speaks YouTube channel and Instagram account, and reads statistics about those videos. It is used only by the jspr.speaks team, only on accounts we own. It does not collect data from viewers or from anyone else's account.

Data the tool accesses

When we sign in to the tool, we grant it access to our own accounts:

  • Google / YouTube: permission to upload videos, read our channel's details and videos, and read YouTube Analytics reports for our channel.
  • Instagram: permission to publish media to our account and read insights about our posts.

The tool uses this access only to publish our videos and to measure how they perform.

Where data is kept

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on our own computer. They are not sent to any server of ours, because we don't run one.
  • Statistics are read when we run a report and kept in local files on that computer.
  • To publish to Instagram, a video file is hosted for a short time on Cloudinary so Instagram can fetch it, and is deleted after it is published.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data obtained through Google or Instagram with anyone. We do not use it for advertising and do not use it to train AI models.

jspr.speaks Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool also uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Google access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Instagram access can be removed in Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites.
  • When access is removed, we delete the stored tokens and any data the tool saved. You can also ask us to delete data by contacting us (below); we act on requests within 30 days.

Visitors to this website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information such as IP addresses; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

The tool is not used by or directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Send a direct message to @jspr.speaks on Instagram.
